Welcome to New Spirit Detox Center, a leading drug and alcohol detox and rehabilitation facility serving the Los Angeles area from its Tarzana, California location. The center provides medically supervised detoxification as the critical first step in recovery, helping clients safely manage withdrawal while laying a stable foundation for lasting sobriety. With a compassionate team and individualized treatment plans, New Spirit Detox Center guides patients through every phase of the recovery process — from detox through ongoing addiction treatment. Families and individuals across the greater LA area trust New Spirit for its evidence-based approach and genuine commitment to healing.

What we do

Medical Detox

Clinically supervised detoxification to safely manage withdrawal from alcohol and drugs.

Residential Inpatient Treatment

Structured live-in rehabilitation providing intensive therapy and around-the-clock support.

Outpatient Treatment Program

Flexible addiction treatment allowing clients to receive care while maintaining daily responsibilities.

Dual Diagnosis Treatment

Integrated care for clients managing both substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions.

Alcohol Rehab

Specialized treatment addressing the physical and psychological dimensions of alcohol dependence.

Drug Rehab

Comprehensive rehabilitation for clients recovering from opioid, stimulant, benzodiazepine, and other substance addictions.
